My Story

Born with
a ball.

I grew up in Morocco, where football isn't just a sport — it's the language everyone speaks. From the streets to the stadiums, the game shaped how I see the world.

I spent 6 years on the pitch, training at the PSG Academy Morocco and playing for FUS Rabat. That grind taught me discipline, reading the game, and understanding what separates good players from great ones.

Since I was 7 years old, there's been one club: FC Barcelona. Not because it was trendy — because the philosophy, the history, the way they play football the right way, that's everything I believe in.

Today I study data and finance. And whenever I can, I bring that lens to football — because the game has never been more quantifiable, and most people still watch it with their eyes closed.
