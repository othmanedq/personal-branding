03
Are Bookmaker Odds Accurate Forecasts?

Co-authored a research paper at Albert School analyzing 20 seasons of Premier League match odds. We tested whether betting company odds are truly accurate forecasts. The result? The market is generally correct—except for away wins. Bookmakers systematically underestimate how often the away team wins, creating a proven betting edge for smart players.
