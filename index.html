TacticAI — DeepMind × Liverpool FC

I analyzed TacticAI, a research paper by Google DeepMind and Liverpool FC applying graph neural networks to corner kick optimization. The finding that stopped me: 1 in 8 goals comes from corners. 30% of professional goals originate from set pieces. Data changes how you read a dead ball situation.

03
Are Bookmaker Odds Rational Forecasts?

Co-authored an academic paper (Albert School × Mines Paris PSL) testing whether Bet365 implied probabilities are rational and efficient forecasts across 20 seasons of Premier League data. We applied ARIMA modelling, Granger causality tests, and the Mincer-Zarnowitz rationality test. The market is broadly efficient — except for away wins, where bookmakers systematically understate the probability (p = 0.028).
